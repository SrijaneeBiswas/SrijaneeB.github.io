I've spent 7+ years building robots that have to work — in warehouses, factory floors, and uncontrolled outdoor environments, across Manufacturing and Aerospace. Graduate school taught me how to solve problems. Toyota taught me how to build things that last — and left me with a quiet conviction that the real measure of good engineering is whether others can trust and operate what you built without you in the room. That thinking shapes how I approach autonomy stacks, deployment infrastructure, and team building alike. I've field-validated GPS-denied navigation against DRDO requirements, shipped CI/CD infrastructure across 10+ international sites, and led teams toward white-box, failure-first testing. I care about mentorship and empowering women in STEM — and I'm now focused on where robotics and ML meet: VLA models, imitation learning, and Physical AI.
